# ISA CPT Course Map

Navigation layer over `references/isa-cpt/`. It tells you **where to look**, not
what the answer is — always open the cited pages to confirm wording before you
rely on a value.

The course is an **ACE-based CPT curriculum**: the ACE Integrated Fitness Training
(IFT) Model is its backbone, screening follows the **ACSM** algorithm, and
programming uses **ACSM/ACE FITT-VP** guidelines. 12 lesson files, one per deck,
`## Page N` = one slide.

### Resistance / Muscular Training Program Design
- **Training principles** — Week 07 Ch 11 p7–8 (specificity; progression = double-progressive; overload ≈5% increments; reversibility; diminishing returns). Also SPORD/SAID — Week 04 Ch 8 p65.
- **Needs assessment** — Week 07 Ch 11 p10.
- **Frequency** — Week 07 Ch 11 p11 (2–3 d/wk; 48–72 h between sessions; inverse to volume/intensity). Per-muscle-group 2–3 d/wk — Table 11-10.
- **Exercise types & selection/order** — Week 07 Ch 11 p12–13 (core/structural/power/assistance; multi-joint before single-joint; alternate upper/lower, push/pull, agonist/antagonist; supersets, compound sets, circuits).
- **Volume / intensity / rest by goal** — Week 07 Ch 11 **Table 9-12, p16**:
  general fitness 1–4 × 8–15, rest 2–3 min, 20–70% 1RM · endurance 2–3 × ≥12, ≤30 s, ≤67% · hypertrophy 3–6 × 6–12, 30–90 s, 67–85% · strength 2–6 × ≤6, 2–5 min, ≥85% · power (single-effort) 3–5 × 1–2, 2–5 min, 80–90% · power (multi-effort) 3–5 × 3–5, 2–5 min, 75–85%.
- **Resistance FITT-VP** — Week 07 Ch 11 **Table 11-10, p29–31** (intensity: 60–70% 1RM novice→intermediate, ≥80% experienced, 40–50% older/sedentary beginners, <50% endurance; reps 8–12 strength/power, 10–15 older beginners, 15–25 endurance; sets 2–4, single set effective for novice/older, ≤2 endurance; rest 2–3 min; progression gradual).
- **Tempo** — Week 07 Ch 11 p17 (1–3 s concentric, 2–4 s eccentric; 4–8 s for strength; ~6 s reps for new exercisers).
- **Progression** — Week 07 Ch 11 p19–20 (bodyweight→machine→free weight; uniplanar→multiplanar; supported→unsupported; isolation→multi-joint; bilateral→unilateral); **2-for-2 rule** & **double progression** p20.
- **Session components** — Week 07 Ch 11 p18 (warm-up with Functional/Movement-phase exercises; conditioning; cool-down).
- **Periodization** — Week 07 Ch 11 p42–46 (macro/meso/microcycle; linear vs undulating; Tables 10-20/10-21; usually only core exercises are periodized). The course's linear example ramps microcycles 12 → 8 → 4 → … → 1 rep and ends with a "goal assessment" week (p45).
- **Strength-block archetype (Russian Strength Program)** — `references/russian-strength-program.md`. A linear-periodization mesocycle for max-strength / PBs on the barbell lifts; maps to **Load/Speed** stage; includes the eligibility gate, the V5 / Classic / Masters variants, fat-loss integration, the scaled entry for beginners, and the retest protocol. Anchored on Ch 11 p42–46, Table 9-12 (p16), Table 11-10 (p29–31), and 1-RM assessment (Ch 10 p69–74).

### ACE IFT Model — phase selection
- **Overview** — Week 02 Ch 2 p7 (the model), Table 2-2 (cardio phases), Table 2-3 (muscular phases).
- **Cardio: Base → Fitness → Performance** — Week 02 Ch 2 p8–9; Week 05 Ch 8 p28–39.
  Base: physically inactive; low–moderate intensity, below talk-test/VT1; 2–3 d/wk, 10–15 min → progress to Fitness when the client can do **≥20 min continuous below the talk-test threshold**.
  Fitness: 3–5 d/wk, ≥20 min; introduce Zone-2 intervals; use the **VT1 test**.
  Performance: endurance-event goals; >30 min, 5–7 d/wk; **VT2 test**; near-/maximal intervals, HIIT; **Rule of 3** for weekly volume.
- **Muscular: Functional → Movement → Load/Speed** — Week 02 Ch 2 p10–14; Week 07 Ch 11 p21–32.
  Functional: re-establish postural stability & kinetic-chain mobility; bodyweight, core, balance; stabilize lumbar region first, then distal segments.
  Movement: the 5 primary movement patterns (push, pull, bend-and-lift, single-leg, rotation) in varied planes; reps over intensity; light load 50–60% 1RM.
  Load/Speed: external load for force/speed; strength/hypertrophy/endurance goals per the resistance guidelines; add power/speed/agility for athletes. Course names its methods as including *"HIIT, plyometrics and power lifting"* (Week 02 Ch 2 p14) — a max-strength block (`references/russian-strength-program.md`) lives here.

### Cardiorespiratory Program Design
- **Evidence-based FITT-VP** — Week 05 Ch 8 **p10** (frequency ≥5 d/wk moderate or ≥3 d/wk vigorous; time 30–60 min moderate / 20–60 min vigorous; volume ≥500–1000 MET-min/wk or ≥150 min/wk or ≥7000 steps/day; pattern continuous or intervals or ≥10-min bouts; progression "start low, go slow").
- **Intensity methods** — Week 05 Ch 8 p11–20 (%MHR, %HRR, RPE, METs, kcal, talk test/VT1, VT2).
- **MHR formulas** — Week 05 Ch 8 p13 (220−age; also 206.9−0.67·age; 208−0.7·age).
- **Karvonen / HRR** — Week 05 Ch 8 p14 (THR = HRR × %intensity + RHR; HRR = MHR − RHR).
- **Three-zone model** — Week 05 Ch 8 p15, p19–20 (Table 8-11): Zone 1 <VT1, 40–60% HRR, RPE 3–4, can talk comfortably · Zone 2 VT1→<VT2, 60–90% HRR, RPE 5–6 · Zone 3 ≥VT2, 90–100% HRR, RPE 7–10.
- **VT1 talk test / VT2 threshold test** — Week 04 Ch 8 p75–76 (VT2 HR = test HR × 0.95).
- **VO₂max field tests** — Week 04 Ch 8 p77–78 (Rockport 1-mile walk; 1.5-mile run — equations on the pages).
- **10% progression rule** — Week 05 Ch 8 p26. **Sedentary-time FITT** — p27.
- **Session components / interval W:R** — Week 05 Ch 8 p41–42.

## Routing examples

These show the *shape* of retrieval — the actual pages are above.

**"How many sets and reps for my beginner client who wants general fitness?"**
1. Training status = novice → Resistance FITT-VP (Week 07 Ch 11 Table 11-10, p29–31).
2. Goal = general fitness → goal-based volume table (Week 07 Ch 11 Table 9-12, p16).
3. IFT muscular phase for a novice → Functional/Movement (Week 02 Ch 2 p10–12; Week 07 Ch 11 p21–28).
4. Progression for later changes → 2-for-2 / double progression (Week 07 Ch 11 p20).

**"Can this 52-year-old with high blood pressure start training?"**
1. ACSM screening algorithm + risk factors (Week 04 Ch 5 p8–9, p14).
2. Signs/symptoms list (Week 04 Ch 5 p7, p10).
3. Hypertension chapter — clearance, medication effects, safety, FITT (Week 10 Ch 13 p15–18; medication effects Week 04 Ch 5 p15–16).
4. Scope of practice / referral (Week 01 Ch 1 p7–8).

**"Build a 3-day/week program for a healthy intermediate lifter wanting to add muscle."**
1. Screen (Ch 5). 2. Goal = hypertrophy → Table 9-12 (3–6 × 6–12, 67–85% 1RM, 30–90 s).
3. Frequency 3 d/wk with 48–72 h spacing (Ch 11 p11) → split logic + exercise order (Ch 11 p13).
4. IFT muscular = Load/Speed, with Functional/Movement in warm-ups (Ch 11 p18, p32).
5. Progression: double progression, ~5% increments (Ch 11 p7, p20); periodization if horizon is long (Ch 11 p42–46).
6. Session components, tempo (Ch 11 p17–18).

**"My client says squats hurt their knee now."**
1. Record the feedback; ask MCQs about pain location, when, severity (pain scale — Ch 15 p88), history.
2. Route to knee conditions & program-design steps (Week 10 Ch 15 p107–115).
3. Healing phases / contraindication if it's a fresh injury (Ch 15 p78); post-rehab table (p89).
4. Decide: regression (partial-ROM squat, Ch 15 p115), substitution, or load/volume change — whichever the notes support. Refer out if pain persists/worsens (scope — Ch 1 p7).
5. Update reasoning in the .md; update the .csv only if the prescription changed.

**"Client dropped from 4 training days to 3."**
1. Update availability in the profile.
2. Re-check frequency guidance (Ch 11 p11; per-muscle-group Table 11-10) and cardio frequency (Ch 8 p10).
3. Re-derive weekly structure (fewer days → consolidate; consider full-body or upper/lower).
4. Update .md strategy + decision log, restructure .csv, re-validate weekly volume against the goal table.

**"I want to run a Russian squat program / peak my lifts / hit a squat PB in 10 weeks."**
1. Screen (Ch 5) — near-maximal lifting is vigorous; flag medical clearance if the algorithm calls for it.
2. Open `references/russian-strength-program.md`; run the **eligibility gate** (§2) — experienced lifter, movement-screen competence (Ch 10 p45–59), loaded-testing contraindications (Ch 10 p69/p72), equipment, recovery.
3. Gate fails (e.g. bare-minimum training background) → **scaled entry** (§6): Movement phase → 60–70% Load/Speed block → re-assess. Don't hand over the archetype.
4. Gate passes → pick variant + wave lifts; goal-band check against Table 9-12 (p16); linear periodization is course-supported for this horizon (Ch 11 p42–46).
5. Fat loss too? → §5: deficit periodisation, conditioning placement, RD referral; general nutrition info only.
6. Retest method (§7): true 1-RM/3-RM (athlete/experienced) vs rep-max → estimated 1-RM (general pop). Generate the CSV with `scripts/russian_block.py`.
