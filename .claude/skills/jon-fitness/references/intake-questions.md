# Client Intake — MCQ Bank

Derived from what the ISA CPT notes say a trainer must establish **before**
screening, assessing, setting goals, and prescribing. This is not a form to read
out top to bottom — use progressive intake, ask what's relevant now, re-derive
after each round.

**Course basis for each stage:**
- Stage 1 ← ACSM preparticipation screening (Ch 5 p6–14), PAR-Q+ (Ch 5 p11),
  signs/symptoms (Ch 5 p7/10), scope & referral (Ch 1 p7–8; Ch 4 p8).
- Stage 2 ← needs assessment (Ch 11 p10), training principles (Ch 11 p7–8),
  goal-based variables (Ch 9 Table 9-12), FITT-VP inputs (Ch 8 p10; Ch 11
  Tables 11-7/8/10), IFT phase selection (Ch 2), lifespan/special-pop triggers
  (Ch 12, 13, 14).
- Stage 3 ← communication & adherence (Ch 3 p20; Ch 4), exercise-selection
  practicality (Ch 11 p12–13), recovery (Ch 11 p48).

Gap priority: **BLOCKING** = can't responsibly build the requested program ·
**IMPORTANT** = can proceed, materially changes the program · **OPTIONAL** =
adherence/fit only.

## Stage 1 — Essential / Screening

### S1. Regular exercise? *(BLOCKING — sets the screening path)*
Do you currently do planned exercise at least 30 min, 3+ days/week, and have for 3+ months?
A. Yes, consistently  B. Some, not consistent  C. No / just starting  D. Returning after a long break

### S2. Diagnosed conditions? *(BLOCKING)*
Has a doctor ever diagnosed you with any of the following? (select all)
A. Heart / cardiovascular disease  B. Metabolic disease (e.g. type 1 or 2 diabetes)  C. Kidney (renal) disease
D. High blood pressure  E. High cholesterol / dyslipidemia  F. A lung condition (asthma, COPD)
G. A bone/joint condition (osteoporosis, arthritis)  H. Other chronic condition (please name)  I. None

### S3. Current signs or symptoms? *(BLOCKING — any yes → medical clearance path)*
Do you currently experience any of these, at rest or with exertion? (select all)
A. Chest pain/discomfort, or pain in neck/jaw/arms  B. Unusual shortness of breath  C. Dizziness or fainting
D. Ankle swelling  E. Rapid/forceful/irregular heartbeat (palpitations)  F. Unusual fatigue with normal activity
G. A known heart murmur  H. None of these

### S4. PAR-Q+ core *(BLOCKING — any yes → follow up, possibly ePARmed-X+ / physician)*
Answer YES/NO:
1. Doctor ever said you have a heart condition or high blood pressure?
2. Chest pain at rest, in daily activity, or during exercise?
3. Loss of balance from dizziness, or lost consciousness in the last 12 months?
4. Ever diagnosed with another chronic medical condition (besides heart disease / high BP)?
5. Currently taking prescribed medication for a chronic condition?
6. Bone, joint, or soft-tissue problem now or in the past 12 months that could be worsened by activity?
7. Doctor ever said you should only do medically supervised physical activity?

### S5. Medications *(IMPORTANT — affects HR/BP response and intensity monitoring)*
Are you on any of: A. Beta blocker  B. Other blood-pressure medication  C. Diuretic
D. Diabetes medication / insulin  E. Other prescription meds (name)  F. None
*(If A or B: HR-based targets may be unreliable — plan to use RPE / talk test. Ch 5 p15–16.)*

### S6. Desired exercise intensity *(BLOCKING — feeds the ACSM algorithm)*
How hard do you intend to train?
A. Light–moderate (can talk comfortably)  B. Moderate–vigorous  C. Vigorous / high-intensity  D. Unsure — advise me

### S7. Life stage / status *(BLOCKING when it triggers a special-population chapter)*
A. Adult, none of the below  B. Under 18 (youth)  C. Pregnant  D. Postpartum (< 6–12 months)
E. Older adult (65+ or significant age-related decline)  F. Prefer to explain

### S8. Current or recent injuries / pain *(BLOCKING for the affected area)*
Any current injury, joint pain, or area that limits movement?
A. None  B. Yes — resolved, cleared by a professional  C. Yes — ongoing / not assessed  D. Recent (< 6 weeks)
*(C or D → get status, refer out if unassessed and painful; program around it per Ch 15.)*

## Stage 2 — Programming

### P1. Primary goal *(BLOCKING — drives every training variable)*
A. Build muscle (hypertrophy)  B. Increase strength  C. Lose body fat  D. Improve cardiovascular fitness
E. General health / fitness  F. Sports / athletic performance  G. Rehab-adjacent function / return to activity  H. Other / unsure

### P2. Secondary goal(s)? *(IMPORTANT)* — same options as P1, or "none".

### P3. Training experience *(BLOCKING — novice vs experienced changes intensity, sets, phase)*
A. New to structured training (< 6 months)  B. Some experience (6 mo – 2 yr), form is developing
C. Experienced (2+ yr), confident with the main lifts  D. Formerly trained, detrained now

### P4. Days per week you can realistically train *(BLOCKING)*
A. 1  B. 2  C. 3  D. 4  E. 5+  F. Varies week to week

### P5. Session length *(BLOCKING)*
A. ~30 min  B. ~45 min  C. ~60 min  D. 75+ min  E. Varies

### P6. Equipment / facility access *(BLOCKING)*
A. Full gym (racks, barbells, machines, cardio)  B. Dumbbells / adjustable weights + bench at home
C. Bands / minimal equipment  D. Bodyweight only  E. Outdoor space (walk/run/bike)  F. Other (describe)

### P7. Cardio equipment available *(IMPORTANT if goal involves cardio)*
A. Treadmill  B. Bike / spin  C. Rower  D. Elliptical / stepper  E. Outdoor run/walk/cycle  F. Pool  G. None

### P8. Movement / technique confidence *(IMPORTANT — gates Load/Speed phase and 1-RM testing)*
Can you perform these with good form unloaded or light? (squat, hinge/deadlift, push, pull, lunge)
A. All of them  B. Most  C. A few  D. Unsure / never taught

### P9. Baseline data available? *(IMPORTANT — needed for HR targets, load selection, tracking)*
A. Resting HR  B. Blood pressure  C. Height / weight / BMI  D. Waist circumference  E. Body composition
F. Any recent lifts / times / distances  G. None — can we assess

### P10. Known contraindication flags for assessments *(IMPORTANT before recommending tests)*
Any of: low-back pain, recent back surgery, shoulder pain, uncontrolled BP, breathing problem
(asthma/COPD), pregnancy, balance impairment, orthopedic limitation?
*(These rule specific assessments in/out — McGill, Y-Balance, 1-RM, VT field tests. Ch 10 / Ch 8.)*

### P11. Program horizon *(IMPORTANT — decides whether to periodize)*
A. 4–6 weeks  B. ~3 months  C. 6+ months  D. Ongoing / open-ended  E. Event on a date (give the date)

### P12. Sport / activity demands *(BLOCKING if P1 = F)*
Which sport, position, season phase, and key physical demands (power, endurance, agility, contact)?

**P13–P16 apply only when P1 = B (strength) or the client asks for a max-strength /
powerlifting-style / "Russian" block.** See `references/russian-strength-program.md`.

### P13. Barbell training background *(BLOCKING for the strength-block archetype — gate G2/G3)*
A. 2+ yr consistent barbell training, confident under load — can state recent working weights
B. 6 mo – 2 yr, still grooving the main lifts
C. < 6 mo / never trained the barbell lifts
D. Trained before, detrained now
*(B/C → scaled entry, not the archetype. Also do the movement screens in B3.)*

### P14. Complex-lift competence *(IMPORTANT — separates the athlete branch from general population)*
Can you perform these with sound technique under moderate load?
A. Power clean / clean  B. Push press / push jerk  C. Front squat / overhead squat
D. None of these — I want basic barbell strength
*(A–C → athlete branch: SAQ / plyometrics / power add-ons available. D → general-population branch.)*

### P15. Current 1-RMs or recent top sets *(BLOCKING to build the wave — drives every load)*
For squat, bench, deadlift, overhead press, weighted pull-up — give either a tested 1-RM or a
recent best set (e.g. "100 kg × 5"). If none: we run a sub-maximal rep test to estimate them
(Table 10-25) — no true 1-RM for a general-population client.

### P16. Retest preference *(IMPORTANT — sets the end-of-block assessment)*
A. Test a true 1-RM / 3-RM at the end (athletes / experienced, no testing contraindications)
B. Just record where I started and retest the same rep-max at the end to see the gain
C. No formal retest — track weekly loads only

### P17. Fat loss alongside the strength block? *(IMPORTANT if P1 = B and P2/goal includes C)*
A. No — strength only  B. Yes, gradual (hold the course's ~1–2 lb/wk)  C. Yes, and I want it faster
*(C → give the trade-offs; a larger deficit is a client/RD nutrition decision, outside the
course's 500–1000 kcal/day band. Nutrition stays general-information only — refer to an RD.)*

## Knowledge-gap loop

```
known info → CPT screening/assessment/programming requirements → list missing items
   → classify BLOCKING / IMPORTANT / OPTIONAL
   → ask prioritized MCQs (BLOCKING first; batch 3–6 at a time)
   → update Client Profile + Outstanding Information in the .md
   → re-derive newly relevant questions (e.g. P12 only if P1=F; P13–P17 only if P1=B
     or a strength-block request; Ch 14 questions if S7≠A)
   → repeat until no BLOCKING gaps remain
   → design the program; leave IMPORTANT/OPTIONAL gaps noted in the plan
```

Ask for as much useful information as is practical, but never ask something that
can't plausibly change the program. Never invent an answer the client didn't give.
