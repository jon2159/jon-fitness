## Deficit Periodisation

_(Only if fat loss runs concurrently — see §5 of the reference. Scope: general nutrition
information only; RD referral logged in Screening.)_

| Weeks | Nutrition target (client's / RD's call) | Conditioning (dosed to deficit + recovery, not a fixed minute target) | Accessory style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accumulation (V5 wk 1–5 / Classic wk 1–3) | moderate deficit (≈ −250 to −500 kcal/d) | Zone 1–2 on off/upper days as recovery allows; steps first | supersets / circuits ok |
| Intensification & peak (V5 wk 6–9 / Classic wk 4–6) | **maintenance** | easy Zone 1 only / steps; cut if recovery is tight | straight sets, lower volume |

_(The Ch 12 "150–250 min/wk" figure is obesity-population guidance — for a lean, trained client
recovery sets the cap, not a minute count. See `references/russian-strength-program.md` §5.)_

- **Chosen approach:** deficit-within-block / sequential blocks
- **If the client wants a faster ("aggressive") rate:** documented trade-offs given
  (lean-mass & strength loss, blunted wave performance, fatigue, adherence); noted that
  a larger deficit is a nutrition decision for the client/RD and sits outside the
  course's 500–1000 kcal/day band. *(Wk09 Ch12 p29–31; General knowledge for anything faster.)*

## Autoregulation Rules

- **RPE cap:** progression-day load at RPE ≥ 9 for 2 sessions running → hold or −5%.
- **Anchor too light:** anchor day ≤ RPE 6 → raise the driving 1-RM.
- **1-RM refresh:** update from rep-max data mid-block; no forced true maxes.
- **Auto-deload:** 2-for-2 fails across a week, or anchor-day RPE creeps → insert an
  anchor-only / −10% microcycle. *(Rationale: periodization = planned recovery, Wk07 Ch11 p42.)*

## Retest Plan

- **Method:** true 1-RM / 3-RM (athlete or experienced, G4 passes) · rep-max → estimated
  1-RM (general population). *(Wk06 Ch10 p69–75)*
- **Baseline recorded:** _(start loads / rep-maxes — so the delta is measurable)_
- **Test-day conditions:** 6–8 h sleep, no vigorous exercise 24 h prior, hydrated
  *(Wk04 Ch8 p70)*; spotting with stable base + neutral spine, rack safeties set
  *(Wk06 Ch10 p74)*; stop-test criteria in effect *(Wk04 Ch8 p71)*.
- **For a competitive athlete:** retest / competition date drives the macrocycle back
  from here.
