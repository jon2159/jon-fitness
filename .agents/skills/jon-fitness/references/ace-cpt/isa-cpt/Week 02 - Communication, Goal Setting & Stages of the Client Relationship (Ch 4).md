## Page 8

RESPOND TO DIFFICULT
DISCLOSURES
- With the disclosure of something very sad, such as a client’s serious illness,
or the illness or death of someone close to the client, a short response may be all that is appropriate
- If a situation affects the exercise program design, turn the conversation back to the practical detail
- If new medical information of concern is disclosed, refer the client to their healthcare provider for medical clearance.
- If a new medical condition places a client outside of the personal trainer’s scope of practice and expertise, the client should be referred out.
