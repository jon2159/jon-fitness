## Page 12

INTENSITY - HEART RATE
- % of MHR or HRR: most widely used for programming and monitoring intensity
- Variables impacting MHR:
- Genetics
- Exercise modality (e.g., MHR varies between running and cycling)
- Medications
- Body size
- Altitude – lowers MHR reached due to a client’s inability to train at higher intensities
- Age – MHR varies significantly among people of the same age

## Page 13

AGE-PREDICTED MHR (APMHR)

- Underestimates MHR in older adults

## Page 27

FITT FOR REDUCING
SEDENTARY TIME
- Frequency
- Take breaks from sedentary time every 60-120 min
- Intensity
- Light intensity: <25 to 50% VO2max, >1.5 to <3.0 METs, or <VT1
- Time
- Limit overall discretionary sitting time to < 2 h/d
- Each break lasts 5-10 min
- Type
- Light activities: e.g. housework, occupational tasks
- Modify environment to limit sitting, e.g. remove chairs
