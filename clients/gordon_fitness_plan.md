## Program Strategy

- **Primary training emphasis (from goal):** Muscular Hypertrophy *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 9-12, p16)*.
- **Cardio IFT phase:** Base Phase *(Wk02 Ch2 p8; Wk05 Ch8 p28)* — 5–10 min dynamic cardiovascular warm-up (<VT1, RPE 3–4) to elevate core temperature and joint lubrication prior to lifting *(Wk05 Ch8 p41)*.
- **Muscular IFT phase:** Initial Movement Phase bridging into Load/Speed Phase *(Wk02 Ch2 p10–14; Wk07 Ch11 p21–32)*.
- **Weekly structure (given 2 days/week):** Two full-body sessions with heavy upper-body volume bias on non-consecutive days (e.g. Tuesday and Friday or Monday and Thursday) to allow 48–72 hours recovery between sessions for each muscle group *(Wk07 Ch11 p11, Table 11-10)*:
  - **Session A:** Upper Horizontal Focus + Anterior Lower + Core
  - **Session B:** Upper Vertical Focus + Posterior Lower + Core
- **Periodization (Macrocycle: 6 months / 24 weeks):** Structured periodization to maximize hypertrophy while preventing overtraining and accommodating connective tissue adaptation *(Wk07 Ch11 p42–46)*:
  - **Mesocycle 1 (Weeks 1–4): Anatomical Adaptation & Movement Competence.** Focus on motor unit recruitment, technique consistency, and connective tissue prep. Variables: 2–3 sets, 10–12 reps, 60–70% 1RM, tempo 3-0-1 (controlled) *(Wk07 Ch11 p17, p29)*.
  - **Mesocycle 2 (Weeks 5–14): Primary Hypertrophy Accumulation.** Progressive overload on core multi-joint lifts. Variables: 3–4 sets, 8–12 reps, 67–80% 1RM, 60–90 s rest *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 9-12, p16)*. Deload planned at Week 10 *(Wk07 Ch11 p47–48)*.
  - **Mesocycle 3 (Weeks 15–24): Advanced Hypertrophy & Overload.** High-density volume and intensified mechanical tension. Variables: 3–5 sets, 6–10 reps, 75–85% 1RM, 90 s rest *(Wk07 Ch11 p16)*. Deload at Week 19; final evaluation and re-assessment at Week 24.

## Exercise Selection Reasoning

### Dynamic upper-body prep (Warm-up & Movement Prep)
- **Purpose:** Elevate tissue temperature, increase synovial fluid in glenohumeral joints, and activate scapular stabilizers *(Wk05 Ch8 p41; Wk07 Ch11 p18)*.
- **Client factors:** Essential injury-prevention and kinetic-chain prep prior to heavy upper body pressing and pulling.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Session components *(Wk05 Ch8 p41)*; movement preparation *(Wk07 Ch11 p18)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Includes arm circles, band pull-aparts, thoracic rotations, and cat-cow to prepare thoracic spine and shoulder girdle.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: add light resistance bands; regression: unloaded active ROM.

### Barbell bench press (Horizontal Push)
- **Purpose:** Primary multi-joint compound movement for pectoralis major, anterior deltoid, and triceps hypertrophy *(Wk03 Ch9 p46; Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Client factors:** Direct driver for upper body mass goal; bilateral stability allows higher mechanical loading *(Wk07 Ch11 p7)*.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Primary movement patterns — Push *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*; multi-joint before single-joint *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Provides greatest load capacity across the horizontal pressing vector.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: increase load via 2-for-2 rule; regression: Dumbbell bench press or push-up if shoulder discomfort occurs *(Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.

### Chest-supported dumbbell row / Chest supported row (Horizontal Pull)
- **Purpose:** Primary multi-joint horizontal pull targeting latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, middle trapezius, and posterior deltoids *(Wk03 Ch9 p49; Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Client factors:** Develops upper-back thickness and balances anterior shoulder loading from heavy pressing *(Wk07 Ch11 p22)*.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Primary movement patterns — Pull *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*; agonist/antagonist balance *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** A chest supported dumbbell row minimizes lumbar spine axial fatigue, preserving recovery when training 2x/week.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Barbell bent-over row; regression: Seated cable row or chest-supported machine row.

### Seated dumbbell overhead press (Vertical Push)
- **Purpose:** Primary compound vertical push targeting anterior/medial deltoids and triceps brachii *(Wk03 Ch9 p46; Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Client factors:** Key exercise for shoulder broadening and shoulder girdle volume.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Shoulder mechanics & planes of motion *(Wk03 Ch9 p46; Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Seated DB variation allows pressing in the scapular plane (~30° anterior to frontal plane), avoiding subacromial impingement *(Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Standing barbell overhead press; regression: Incline dumbbell press if full vertical overhead mobility is restricted *(Wk06 Ch10 p33; Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.

### Lat pulldown (Vertical Pull)
- **Purpose:** Multi-joint vertical pull emphasizing latissimus dorsi, teres major, and biceps brachii *(Wk03 Ch9 p49; Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Client factors:** Builds upper body V-taper and back width.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Primary movement patterns — Pull *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Allows precise load calibration to maintain 8–12 rep hypertrophy target compared to bodyweight pull-ups.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Weighted pull-up / chin-up; regression: Assisted pull-up or band-assisted pull-up.

### Dumbbell lateral raise (Upper-Body Isolation)
- **Purpose:** Single-joint isolation targeting lateral deltoid for shoulder cap hypertrophy *(Wk03 Ch9 p46; Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Client factors:** Direct priority for upper-body width and aesthetics.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Single-joint exercises programmed after multi-joint *(Wk07 Ch11 p13, Table 11-10)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Directly isolates the lateral deltoid which receives sub-maximal stimulus from compound presses alone.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Cable lateral raise (constant tension); regression: Light DB raises with reduced lever arm.

### Incline dumbbell bicep curl (Arm Hypertrophy)
- **Purpose:** Single-joint elbow flexor isolation emphasizing the long head of the biceps brachii via shoulder hyperextension stretch *(Wk03 Ch9 p49)*.
- **Client factors:** Gordon's upper body growth priority.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Single-joint accessory volume *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Incline bench places biceps in a stretched position for enhanced hypertrophy stimulus.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Standing barbell curl; regression: Standing hammer curl.

### Cable overhead triceps extension (Arm Hypertrophy)
- **Purpose:** Single-joint elbow extensor isolation emphasizing the long head of the triceps brachii *(Wk03 Ch9 p46)*.
- **Client factors:** Triceps account for ~60% of upper arm mass; crucial for upper body growth.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Single-joint accessory volume *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Overhead shoulder flexion pre-stretches the triceps long head.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Dip or skullcrusher; regression: Cable triceps pushdown.

### Goblet squat (Lower Body Compound & Core)
- **Purpose:** Bend-and-lift foundational movement pattern targeting quadriceps, glutes, and anterior core stability *(Wk02 Ch2 p12; Wk03 Ch9 p52)*.
- **Client factors:** Maintains balanced lower-body strength and trunk stability without excessive spinal compression.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Bend-and-lift movement pattern *(Wk02 Ch2 p12; Wk06 Ch10 p38)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Anterior load placement promotes an upright torso and deep knee flexion while reinforcing core bracing.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Barbell front squat or back squat; regression: Box squat or bodyweight squat.

### Romanian deadlift (Posterior Chain & Core)
- **Purpose:** Hip-hinge movement pattern targeting hamstrings, gluteus maximus, and erector spinae *(Wk02 Ch2 p12; Wk03 Ch9 p52)*.
- **Client factors:** Builds posterior chain strength to stabilize heavy upper-body lifts and balance quad dominant work.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Hip hinge pattern *(Wk02 Ch2 p12; Wk10 Ch15 p106)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Eccentric hamstring loading builds hip stability and strengthens spinal erectors.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progression: Barbell conventional deadlift; regression: Dumbbell Romanian deadlift or kettlebell deadlift from riser.

### Static chest and shoulder stretch (Cool-down)
- **Purpose:** Restore resting muscle length to pectoralis major and anterior deltoids post-pressing *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 11-7, p25)*.
- **Client factors:** Counters internal shoulder rotation and post-workout tightness.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Flexibility FITT-VP *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 11-7, p25)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Doorway or corner pec stretch held for 20–30 seconds at mild discomfort.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progress to PNF contract-relax if assisted.

### Static lat and hamstring stretch (Cool-down)
- **Purpose:** Elongate latissimus dorsi, thoracolumbar fascia, and posterior chain musculature *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 11-7, p25)*.
- **Client factors:** Enhances recovery and relieves spinal tension after vertical pulling and hip hinging.
- **Relevant ISA CPT section:** Flexibility FITT-VP *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 11-7, p25)*.
- **Reason for this exercise / variation:** Overhead pole lat stretch and standing/seated hamstring stretch held for 20–30 seconds.
- **Progression / regression options:** Progress to longer holds or active-isolated stretching.

## Progression Strategy

- **Method:** Double progression protocol combined with the 2-for-2 rule *(Wk07 Ch11 p7, p20)*:
  1. Maintain a given load until Gordon can perform the top of the target rep range (e.g., 12 reps) with proper technique across all assigned sets.
  2. Apply the **2-for-2 rule**: If Gordon completes ≥2 reps over the assigned target on the final set for 2 consecutive workouts, advance load.
- **Resistance load increments:** Increase load by ~5% for upper-body lifts and 5–10% for lower-body compound lifts *(Wk07 Ch11 p7; Wk04 Ch8 p65)*.
- **Deload / recovery plan:** Planned deload every 5th to 6th week (e.g., Week 5, Week 10, Week 15, Week 20), reducing volume by 40–50% (2 sets instead of 3–4) and maintaining intensity at ~70% to facilitate central nervous system and soft-tissue recovery *(Wk07 Ch11 p47–48)*.

## Monitoring & Reassessment

- **Track each session:** Record exercises, sets, reps completed, external load, and session RPE (0–10 scale per Wk04 Ch8 p19).
- **Reassess:**
  - Circumference measurements (chest, shoulders, upper arms) every 6 weeks *(Wk04 Ch7 p16–18)*.
  - Movement quality and joint comfort reviewed weekly *(Wk06 Ch10; Wk10 Ch15)*.
- **Stop / refer signals:**
  - Acute or sharp joint pain (especially shoulder impingement pain, elbow tendonitis, or acute lower back strain) → immediately cease exercise, apply Ch 15 pain-free ROM principles, and refer to medical provider / PT if persistent *(Wk10 Ch15 p78, p89, p92)*.
  - Exertional dizziness, chest tightness, or shortness of breath → terminate session immediately and refer to physician *(Wk04 Ch5 p8–10; Wk04 Ch8 p71)*.
