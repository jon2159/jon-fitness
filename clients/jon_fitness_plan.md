## Autoregulation Rules

- **RPE cap:** If the progression-day load hits RPE >= 9 for **two sessions running**, hold the load or drop ~5%.
- **Anchor too light:** If the anchor day sits <= RPE 6 consistently, nudge the driving 1-RM up ~2.5 kg.
- **1-RM refresh mid-block:** Update the driving 1-RM from rep-max data rather than forcing true maxes before Week 9.
- **Auto-deload:** If 2-for-2 fails across a whole week, or anchor-day RPE creeps upward week-over-week → insert an anchor-only / -10% microcycle. *(Rationale: periodization's main benefit is planned physical & mental recovery, Wk07 Ch11 p42.)*
- **Applied from the course:** RPE as an intensity metric *(Wk05 Ch8 p15–20)*; double-progression / 2-for-2 logic *(Wk07 Ch11 p7, p20)*.

## Exercise Selection Reasoning

### Session A Wave Lifts — Back Squat and Bench Press

- **Purpose:** Primary lower-body strength (squat = bend-and-lift pattern) and upper-body horizontal push (bench) — two of the five wave lifts.
- **ISA CPT basis:** Multi-joint, axial-loaded compound exercises are the recommended *core / structural* exercises for periodization: *"typically only core exercises (major muscles, multi-joint) are periodized"* *(Wk07 Ch11 p42)*. Five movement patterns: squat = bend-and-lift, bench = push *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*.
- **Progression / regression:** Wave loading per the V5 table. If form breaks under high load, drop to a submaximal hold week (anchor-only) and reassess.

### Session B Wave Lifts — Deadlift, Military Press, Weighted Pull-up

- **Purpose:** Posterior-chain hinge (deadlift), vertical push (OHP), and vertical pull (pull-up) — remaining three wave lifts covering all five primary patterns across Sessions A and B.
- **ISA CPT basis:** All five patterns across A+B: push (bench + OHP), pull (pull-up), bend-and-lift (deadlift + squat), rotation in warm-up *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*.

### Session A Accessories — Bulgarian Split Squat and Dips

- **Purpose:** Unilateral single-leg pattern (addresses L/R symmetry, glute and quad hypertrophy); dips = chest/tricep finishing volume.
- **ISA CPT basis:** Single-leg movement *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*; accessories not on the wave at fixed 3x8–12 *(Wk07 Ch11 p42)*.

### Session B Accessories — Romanian Deadlift and Face Pulls

- **Purpose:** RDL = hamstring/posterior-chain hypertrophy volume; face pulls = rear delt, external rotator, and scapular retractor health — critical for shoulder longevity given pressing volume *(Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.
- **ISA CPT basis:** Agonist/antagonist balance (press volume offset with horizontal pulls) *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*; MSK shoulder region steps: strengthen scapular stabilizers *(Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.

### Warm-up (all sessions)

- **Movement-phase warm-up: 5 patterns + ramp sets to first work load** — bodyweight to light, 8–10 min before all strength sessions *(Wk07 Ch11 p18)*.
- **Movement warm-up + shoulder mobility (band pull-aparts, face pull, arm circles)** — used on Wednesday pump day to prime pressing muscles and rear delts before volume work *(Wk07 Ch11 p18)*.

### Cool-down (all sessions)

- **Static stretch: hip flexors, hamstrings, pecs, lats** — 2 reps/side, hold 20–30 s, 5 min after Sessions A and B. Flexibility FITT-VP Table 11-7 *(Wk07 Ch11 p25)*.
- **Static stretch: pecs / front delts / lats / biceps / hamstrings** — extended 8-min flexibility cool-down after Wednesday pump day *(Wk07 Ch11 p25)*.

### Wednesday Pump Day (Session C) — Exercise List

- **Purpose:** A dedicated hypertrophy session targeting chest, shoulders, arms, and posterior chain. No barbell compound lifts competing with the strength days.
- **ISA CPT basis:** Hypertrophy prescription 3–6 × 6–12 @ 67–85%, 30–90 s rest *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 9-12, p16)*; exercise selection from all 5 movement patterns in varied planes *(Wk02 Ch2 p12)*; multi-joint before single-joint *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.

**Exercises (in session order):**

1. **Incline Dumbbell Press** — 4 × 8–12 @ RPE 7–8. Upper chest emphasis; push pattern; multi-joint first *(Wk07 Ch11 p13; Wk02 Ch2 p12)*.
2. **Cable Row (Seated or Single-arm)** — 4 × 10–12 @ RPE 7–8. Pull pattern; agonist/antagonist press offset *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
3. **Dumbbell Shoulder Press (Seated)** — 3 × 10–12 @ RPE 7–8. Vertical push; shoulder hypertrophy *(Wk07 Ch11 Table 9-12, p16)*.
4. **Cable Lateral Raise** — 3 × 12–15 @ RPE 7–8. Shoulder width; single-joint after multi-joint *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
5. **Cable Rear Delt Fly** — 3 × 12–15 @ RPE 7–8. Scapular stabilizer / posterior shoulder health *(Wk10 Ch15 p92)*.
6. **EZ-Bar or Dumbbell Curl** — 3 × 10–12 @ RPE 7–8. Biceps hypertrophy; single-joint isolation *(Wk07 Ch11 p13)*.
7. **Tricep Rope Pushdown** — 3 × 12–15 @ RPE 7–8. Triceps hypertrophy; single-joint isolation.
8. **Cable Pull-Through or Hamstring Curl** — 3 × 12–15 @ RPE 7–8. Posterior chain flush on pump day; does not compete with deadlift recovery *(Wk07 Ch11 p47–48)*.

## Progression Strategy

- **Wave lifts:** Linear week-over-week per the V5 schedule (fixed anchor @ 80%; progression day increases reps then intensity). Applied from the course: linear periodization = volume down / intensity up *(Wk07 Ch11 p44)*.
- **Accessories:** Double progression — work up the rep range (8 to 12) at a fixed load, then increase load ~5% and drop back to 8 reps *(Wk07 Ch11 p7, p20)*.
- **Wednesday pump day:** Same double-progression logic.
- **Deload:** Auto-deload via RPE / 2-for-2 failure criteria (see Autoregulation Rules). No scheduled deload needed in the V5 structure as the anchor day serves as the built-in lighter exposure.
- **Between mesocycles:** After Week 9 retest, update 1-RMs from the test results and run the next V5 block with the new numbers. If plateau, consider the Classic 6-week variant for single-lift peaking.

## Monitoring & Reassessment

- **Track each session:** Load lifted, reps completed (vs target), RPE on each wave set, notes on how it felt. Anchor-day RPE is the key recovery indicator.
- **Reassess:**
  - Week 5 (mid-block): review anchor-day RPE trend; update driving 1-RM from any rep-max data.
  - Week 9: full 1-RM retest on all five wave lifts.
  - After 2–3 mesocycles: girths (chest, arm, shoulder) for hypertrophy tracking.
- **Stop / refer signals:** Any chest pain, dizziness, or palpitations at rest or during exercise → stop, refer to physician immediately *(Wk04 Ch8 p71)*. Joint pain that appears or worsens during the program → stop the offending movement, reassess via Ch 15 region steps *(Wk10 Ch15 p78)*.
